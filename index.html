Hexplore

Turn-based hex tactics · roguelike

Lay down lava, push the enemy in, freeze it, blow it up. In Hexplore, you craft the battlefield with alchemy, then turn it against your foes.

  • No luck on your side: every turn is a little puzzle.
  • 45-minute runs, ten floors, and a story that moves on even when you lose.
  • Creatures more clumsy than evil, and heroes who tease each other.

Who’s behind the paws?

RedPaws Studio is just one person. A day job by day; video games in the evenings and on weekends.

I’ve played games all my life and I want to share that joy: warm worlds, expressive characters, and rules you get in one sentence and dig into for hours.

And the red panda? That’s Rouxy, the mascot: endlessly curious, a little clumsy, always ready to explore. Pretty much the studio’s motto.
